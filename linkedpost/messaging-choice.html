Architect's takeaway — ask these first

Do I need streaming or messaging?
What is my real throughput, at peak?
Do I need replay of past events?
How much operational complexity?
Do I need queues and DLQ?
Who operates this at 2am?
Do I need transactions & ordering?
What does the business need?

THE REAL TAKEAWAY
