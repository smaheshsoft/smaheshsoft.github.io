  • Lower blast radius
  • Real production validation
  • Gradual rollout
  • Excellent for high-traffic systems
  • More complex traffic management
  • Requires strong observability
  • DB/Schema compatibility becomes critical
WHEN TO USE WHAT?
🎯

CHOOSE BLUE-GREEN WHEN

  • You need a fast, predictable cutover
  • Easy rollback is a priority
  • Major releases / platform upgrades
  • You can manage dual environments
🎯

CHOOSE CANARY WHEN

  • You want to validate with real users
  • Minimize blast radius is critical
  • High-traffic, customer-facing systems
  • You have strong monitoring & alerting
